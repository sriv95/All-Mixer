Split instrumental (drums)

A second model takes the drums out of the instrumental, so Drums and Others get their own sliders (each one scales the Instrumental slider). It needs a one-time download, more GPU, and adds about 4 s of audio delay. With High or Best quality it also needs a fast GPU, and the delay limit goes up to at least 8 s.
